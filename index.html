Features

Everything around your prayer, in one quiet app.

From Fajr to Isha — fard, sunnah, shaf' and witr — with times, direction and history woven in.

It watches, you pray

A neural pose model on the phone itself reads each position and walks the exact sequence of every rakah — qiyam, ruku', two sujoods, jalsa — and knows when the first tashahhud arrives. A gentle vibration marks each completed rakah. If the AI ever misses a movement, one tap saves the full prayer.

Qibla compass

A live compass points to the Kaaba from wherever you are, shows the exact bearing and your distance in kilometres — with a soft haptic when you're aligned.

How it works

Ready in four steps — set up once, pray forever.

The only setup is recording your ruku' the first time. After that, one tap before every prayer.

Phone flat · camera up

Place your phone

Lay it flat on the floor in front of your prayer spot, front camera facing up toward you. The app checks placement and tells you when it can see your whole body.

~5 seconds

Record your ruku'

Bow once and hold for about two seconds. The app learns your bowing posture — not a generic one — so counting fits your body, your mat, your room.

One-time · per person

Start and pray

Tap Start; a calm 10-second countdown lets you settle into qiyam. Then simply pray — a gentle vibration confirms each completed rakah.

10 s countdown

Stop and save

Tap Stop when you finish. If a movement was missed, one tap still saves the complete prayer. Every record keeps its date, start time, duration and rakaat on your calendar.

Saved instantly · offline

In the app: Settings → Profile → Delete account — this wipes your cloud backup, removes the local data and signs you out on every device. You can also remove the hidden backup from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → KamRakaa → Delete hidden app data. Guest data disappears the moment you clear the app's storage.

Privacy

Your prayer is private. So is your data.

On-device AI

Pose detection runs entirely on your phone — the model never leaves the device. Camera frames are analysed in memory and discarded: never saved, never uploaded, never seen by a server. The camera is only active while you're counting.

Your backup is yours

If you sign in, your history syncs to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive — a single file only this app can see, under the restricted drive.appdata scope. Guest mode never touches the cloud at all.

Delete your data: in the app, go to Settings → Profile → Delete account, or remove it from Google Drive → Settings (gear) → Manage apps → KamRakaa → Delete hidden app data.
