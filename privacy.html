Privacy Policy

KamRakaa (كام ركعة) · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the KamRakaa mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") uses, where it is stored, and the choices you have. The app counts the rakahs of your prayer using your phone's camera and on-device AI, saves your prayer history, and shows prayer times and the Qibla direction.

In short: we run no servers and have no access to your data. Camera images are analysed on your phone and are never saved or uploaded. If you sign in with Google, your prayer history is backed up to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, which only you and the app can reach. We show no ads and use no analytics or tracking.

2. Information the app uses

Information Why it is used Where it goes
Camera images (front camera, live video frames) To detect your body posture (standing, bowing, prostration) and count rakahs. Processed in real time on your device by an on-device AI model. Never recorded, saved, or transmitted. The camera runs only while you are calibrating or counting a prayer.
Location (approximate or precise, only while the app is open) To calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and to show your city name. Prayer times and Qibla are computed on your device. The last known location and city name are cached on your device so the app works offline. To turn coordinates into a city name, the app uses your phone's operating-system geocoding service, which on Android is provided by Google and may send the coordinates to Google (see section 5). We do not receive your location.
Prayer history (prayer type, date, start and end time, duration, number of rakahs counted) To show your history, calendar and details screens. Stored on your device. If you sign in with Google, also backed up to your Google Drive (section 4).
Google account profile (name, email address, profile photo, account ID), only if you choose "Continue with Google" To identify which account owns the data, show your profile, and run the Drive backup. Kept on your device only. Not sent to us.
Motion / compass sensor (magnetometer) To point the Qibla needle. Read live on your device. Not stored or sent anywhere.
App settings (language, theme, calculation method, madhab) To remember your choices. Stored on your device only.

We do not collect your contacts, files, microphone audio, advertising ID, or payment information.

3. Guest mode

You can use the app as a guest without signing in. In guest mode everything stays on your device only. If you later sign in with Google, the app asks whether you want to merge your guest history into that account. Guest data is never uploaded unless you choose to merge it.

4. Google sign-in and Google Drive backup

If you sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission: drive.appdata (access to the app's own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive). Through it, the app:

  • stores one small file containing your prayer history (and markers for deleted entries) so your history can be synced between your devices and restored on a new phone;
  • cannot see, read, or change any of your other Drive files;
  • sends data only directly between your phone and Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We run no intermediate server.

The hidden folder is not visible in your normal Drive, and we (the developer) cannot access it. Sync runs when you open the app, when your phone reconnects to the internet, after you save or delete a prayer, and occasionally in the background. Google handles that data under its own Privacy Policy.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the backup and sync feature you see in the app. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, and do not allow humans to read it.

5. Third parties and sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.
  • Google provides sign-in, Google Drive storage (the backup you control), and on Android the location-to-city lookup. These are provided directly between you and Google.
  • AI model download: the on-device pose-detection model is downloaded once from a public model-hosting server when first needed. The request contains no personal data beyond what every internet request reveals, such as your IP address.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

6. Storage, retention and security

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Your Drive backup is kept until you delete your account data in the app (section 7) or remove it yourself in Google Drive.
  • Network connections use HTTPS. Sign-in sessions are managed by Google's sign-in system; the app does not store your password and does not keep Google access tokens in its own storage.
  • No method of storage is perfectly secure, but because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot lose or leak it from our side.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete from the app: Settings → Profile → Delete account (visible when signed in with Google). This deletes your backup from Google Drive, erases your prayers and cached location from the device, disconnects the app from your Google account, and signs you out. It requires an internet connection. Your Google account itself is not deleted.
  • Delete individual prayers or whole days from the History screen at any time.
  • Without the app: remove the hidden data in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → KamRakaa → Delete hidden app data, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Permissions: you can turn off camera or location access in your phone's settings. Camera access is required for counting prayers; location access is required for prayer times and Qibla.
  • Other devices: deleting on one phone does not erase copies already on other phones signed into the same account. Sign out or delete on those phones too.

Step-by-step instructions are also on our account deletion page.

8. Sensitive nature of prayer data

A record of your prayers can reveal religious practice. For this reason we keep it only on your device and, if you choose to sign in, in your own private Google Drive storage. We never access, analyse, or share it.

9. Children

The app is a general-audience religious utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we receive no data at all, a parent or guardian can manage everything by deleting the data on the device or in the child's own Google account.
